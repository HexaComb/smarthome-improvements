## What actually moves the price

**1. Retrofit vs full-frame.** Nearly every Fresno stucco home is a candidate
for retrofit (insert) installation: the new window goes into your existing
frame, the stucco stays intact, and the install is clean and fast. Full-frame
replacement, where everything comes out down to the studs, adds roughly 30 to
50 percent for stucco cutting, patching, and paint. You only need it when the
old frame is rotted or badly out of square, and an honest assessment will tell
you which situation you have.

**2. The glass package.** In Climate Zone 13 heat, glass is the whole game.
Dual-pane Low-E glass is the code-required baseline under
[Title 24](https://www.energy.ca.gov/programs-and-topics/programs/building-energy-efficiency-standards),
and it is what blocks summer heat before it enters the room. Upgrades that add
cost: sound-dampening glass near busy streets, obscure glass for bathrooms,
and tempered glass, which the building code requires near doors, floors, and
tubs whether you like it or not.

**3. Style.** Horizontal sliders are the workhorse and the best value. Single
and double hung cost a little more. Casements, bays, and big picture windows
cost the most because there is more frame, more glass, and more labor per
opening.

**4. Access and stories.** Second-story openings and tight side yards slow a
crew down, and that shows up in labor.

**5. The brand tier.** Builder-grade vinyl exists to hit a price. Premium
lines cost more up front and make it back in glass performance, frame
strength, and warranty. We install [Anlin](/windows/window-replacement/),
which is built about 15 minutes from us in Clovis and carries a double
lifetime warranty that transfers to the next owner of your home.

## What a real quote should include

When you compare bids, make sure each one covers all of this, or the cheap
bid is not actually cheap:

- Permit and Title 24 compliance (required in Fresno and Clovis, no exceptions)
- Removal and disposal of the old windows
- New screens, exterior sealing, and interior trim or caulk lines
- Tempered glass wherever code requires it
- The warranty in writing: product, glass, and labor

That last line matters more than people think. A lifetime product warranty is
worthless if the company that installed it disappears, which is one more
reason to hire a licensed local contractor you can call in five years.

## What new windows give back

We will not promise your windows "pay for themselves," because that depends
on your house, your habits, and PG&E's rates. What is real:

- The EPA's ENERGY STAR program estimates replacing single-pane windows saves
  roughly $100 to $580 per year nationally, and Fresno's cooling season sits
  at the expensive end of that math.
- The federal 25C tax credit returns 30 percent of qualifying window costs, up
  to $600 per year.
- Comfort is immediate: rooms stop swinging hot, street noise drops, and
  single-pane condensation disappears.
- Appraisers and buyers in our market treat new dual-pane windows as a real
  upgrade, especially on 1970s to 1990s tract homes still wearing original
  aluminum frames.
