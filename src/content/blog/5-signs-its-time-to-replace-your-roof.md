Your roof plays a crucial role in protecting your home, but over time, wear and tear can take its toll. Knowing when to replace your roof is essential to avoiding costly damage and ensuring your family's safety.

Here are five key signs that indicate it's time for a new roof.

## 1. Missing or Damaged Shingles

If you notice shingles that are missing, cracked, or curling, it's a clear indication that your roof is nearing the end of its lifespan. Damaged shingles expose your home to water leaks and structural issues, so acting quickly is essential.

## 2. Sagging Roof

A sagging roof is a major red flag. It often points to structural damage caused by prolonged moisture exposure or inadequate support. If you see sagging areas, it's time to call a professional for an inspection.

## 3. Leaks and Water Damage

Water stains on your ceiling or walls are a telltale sign of roof leaks. Left untreated, these leaks can lead to mold growth, wood rot, and other expensive repairs.

## 4. Granule Loss

If you see granules in your gutters or around your home, it's a sign that your shingles are deteriorating. Granules protect your roof from UV rays, and their loss means your roof is less effective.

## 5. Age of the Roof

Most roofs last 20-25 years, depending on the material and installation quality. If your roof is approaching or exceeding this age, consider a replacement to maintain your home's integrity.
